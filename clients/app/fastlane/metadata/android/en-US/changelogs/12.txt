- Android: swipe a note card right (as well as left) to archive it.
- Desktop: keyboard shortcuts: Ctrl+F focuses search, Ctrl+E toggles preview, Ctrl+Shift+A archives and Ctrl+Shift+Del trashes the open note; Ctrl+/ or F1 lists them all. Ctrl+N and these shortcuts now also work on Windows.
- Editor toolbar: Archive and Move to Trash now live in a three-dot overflow menu next to Note color and Preview.
